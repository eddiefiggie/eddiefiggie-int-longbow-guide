Heroic Calamitous listed-ingredient subtotal (derived): 25 each of Bleak Alternators, Conductors, Insulators and Resistors (5 + 5 + 10 + 5), plus 20 Bleak Wires. This adds only the listed ingredients for the four purchases in the heroic Calamitous row below, not all possible costs or current availability.

Heroic Viktranium Experiment crafting — separate purchases; “parts” below means Bleak Alternators, Conductors, Insulators and Resistors.
  • Blank Calamitous Longbow: 5 of each part; no Bleak Wires listed.
  • Heroic Melancholic Flames (Weapon): 5 of each part; no Bleak Wires listed.
  • Heroic Dolorous Flames (Weapon): 10 of each part; no Bleak Wires listed.
  • Heroic Miserable Armor-Piercing (Weapon): 5 of each part plus 20 Bleak Wires.
The captured crafting table warns that some heroic Dolorous augments are named Dreadful in game and marks Flames “Heroic - Dreadful”; the heroic item page calls this selected augment Dolorous Flames. Before changing a Lamordia augment

Use this checklist when filling the heroic Calamitous or changing the crafted endgame bow. The retained crafting page (revision 720728) distinguishes slot compatibility, item level and recovery:

  1. Match the full slot type. Compare the augment tooltip’s Augment Type with the item’s slot. The family and variant must match: Melancholic (Weapon) fits Melancholic Slot (Weapon), not Melancholic Slot (Armor). Use Weapon augments for the bow’s Lamordia weapon slots.
  2. Keep a levelling bow usable. Heroic slots accept Legendary augments and vice versa, but a Legendary augment raises a heroic item’s minimum level to 34. For the heroic Calamitous route, choose the heroic versions in the recipe above; fitting the slot alone does not make an augment suitable for your level.
  3. Recover before replacing if you want to keep the old augment. Placing a new augment in an occupied slot can destroy the slotted augment. A Viktranium Pick removes a Lamordia augment and returns it unharmed for use elsewhere; a Jeweler’s Tool Kit does not work. Check that you have the Pick before changing a fill you intend to keep.

Finding a Pick: the retained page lists rare finds in Chill of Ravenloft chests, DDO Store purchase, and possible Chill of Ravenloft Saga rewards. It does not give a drop rate or guarantee a reward. These are retained crafting instructions, not a fresh live-game check or a claim about ordinary colored augment slots.
