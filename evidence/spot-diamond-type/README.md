## Application and limits

The own-item excerpt supports this variant's Competence label. Applying the
receipt's same-type rule to Spot means multiple Competence bonuses to Spot do
not add: only the highest applies. The receipt preserves named Tailwind and
Marksmanship exceptions in their own scopes; neither establishes a Spot exception.

Naming other skill bonus types is not positive stacking evidence. This correction
does not establish Competence/Morale/Luck compatibility, a final Spot subtotal,
a final +22 skill gain or trap success. Acquisition remains unknown.
