Optional early XP: retained Wiki checks

The Guide’s optional tips suggest saving received Daily Dice XP gems and using available gems toward levels 1–3, with The Master’s Gift as a separate optional goal. Saving and choosing when to spend tokens are build advice, not a Wiki finding about the optimal level range or guaranteed storage. Neither gems nor the Gift are assumed owned.

Limits and gaps

  • These are observations from 5 November 2026. Revision IDs and capture times do not establish current live-game behavior. No new source retrieval or in-game test was performed for this change; known Wiki access restrictions were not retried or bypassed.
  • The source explains awarded tokens and consumption. It does not establish expiry, indefinite storage, bank behavior, cross-life persistence or transfer procedure. The saving recommendation gives no guarantee about those things; available gems are the condition for use.
  • The level range is a player recommendation. No XP threshold, roll count, expected yield, guaranteed skip, epic/reaper benefit or maximum-XP rationale is asserted.
  • The Gift recipe is a list of required ingredients, not evidence of easy or immediate access. Direct ingredient/altar page bodies are not supplied here, so quest routes, quest levels, pack ownership, grind and new-player availability remain unverified. Readers can leave it as a later optional goal.
  • The Gift’s ML1 does not establish a particular early host item. No named early slot, socket swap, removal method, reuse or stacking with other earned-XP bonuses is selected or certified. The existing ML10 belt selections and unresolved early kit remain unchanged.

Regression scope

tools/test_early_xp.py reads the retained source itself and checks exact identity, timestamp and whole-page text hashes before binding the reviewed facts and qualifications to the actual Guide paragraphs. Negative controls cover eligibility, yield promises, roll banking, token-bonus interaction, missing or hidden cautions and an unsupported claim appended outside the scoped block. A scoped guard scans all active visible text for Daily Dice, XP gems/tokens, The Master’s Gift or “the Gift”, excluding only the reviewed tips and two exact existing Gift cells. Changing the surrounding HTML tag cannot bypass it. It is not a general detector for paraphrased claims or CSS visibility. The 56 named prior snapshots are protected while later snapshots remain allowed. Release review separately compares the entire baseline Guide and protected audit/evidence/model files. These are source and document checks, not browser visibility or game tests.
