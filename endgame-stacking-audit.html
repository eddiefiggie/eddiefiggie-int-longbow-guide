Abilities or skills that stack add their effects together. For example, modifiers of +1 Save all and +2 Save against fear stack to create a total +3 to Save versus fear (in addition to the +1 to Save against everything else). In most cases however, only the strongest effect applies. For example, having non-stacking +3 and +5 modifiers is only worth a +5 bonus, not +8.

Bonus Types & Stacking[edit]

Bonuses usually (but not always) have a keyword, such as enhancement or resistance, identifying what type of bonus it is. Players usually try to maximize the potential of their characters in a given area by giving them a collection of bonus with different keywords. Achieving a high Armor Class is a good example of this strategy. Support Veterans

Stacking follows a few predictable general rules:

Bonuses of the same type do not stack:
An enhancement bonus to AC from armor is an Armor bonus. The enhancement bonus to AC from a shield is a shield bonus. In this case, the two bonuses to AC do stack.
When an armor bonus to AC is granted by something other than armor or a docent (such as Black Widow Bracers), it does not stack with an actual armor.
When a Shield bonus to AC comes from something other than a shield (such as the Shield (spell)), it does not stack with an actual shield.
Untyped bonuses stack. Note that a bonus without a keyword isn't necessarily an untyped bonus, the description might just be lacking.
Penalties always stack.
Feat bonuses stack. A character with Lightning Reflexes (+2 bonus to Reflex saves) and Luck of Heroes (+1 bonus to all saves) will have a +3 Reflex save.
Bonuses from character Enhancements that don't mention a specific bonus almost always stack. They are not automatically "enhancement bonuses."
Note: Character enhancements with exactly the same name do not stack, other than the T3 & T4 Ability score enhancements.
Benefits from the same family, like Fortification or False Life, do not stack.
Damage Reduction does not stack (e.g. Stoneskin), except for the added DR that comes from active blocking (therefore it's not a waste to use it together with Barkskin which grants an increase to AC, i.e. an unrelated modifier).
Example[edit]

If you use a bow or crossbow, bonuses and properties on your weapon do not stack with ones from the same category on your ammunition. For example, using +3 bolts of lesser undead bane in a +1 flaming crossbow of lesser undead bane gives your shots +3 to attack and damage (not +4), flaming damage, and lesser undead bane effect once. MRR is applied to the damage that passes through the resistance and this is always rounded down. (20 - 10) * (100 - 37.5)% = 6.25 round down to 6

100/(100+Rating) Example: A character is hit for 100 points of damage.

100 MRR takes 50% less damage, 50 points.

When a critical hit is negated, damage is applied normally rather than at the critical hit multiplier.
Weapon enchantments that trigger on a critical hit (e.g. Holy Burst, Bloodletter or Radiance) continue to work even if the critical hit itself is negated.
When a sneak attack is negated, the extra damage is not applied.

In DDO, most players strive for roughly +10% fortification per level, with the most durable characters achieving +300% at level 30. Most monsters don't have fortification, unless it is granted by the race/type (e.g. Undead, Constructs). Quest and raid bosses have higher fortification, but usually not full.

Players and monsters can gain abilities to bypass a portion of fortification.

Fortification on Monsters[edit]
Any monster that belongs to a type or race that are considered "immune to critical hits" (Undead, Oozes, Constructs and Elementals) gains a natural Fortification of 100%. This also applies to summoned creatures and pets.
Multiple Monster Champion types gain bonuses to Fortification depending on their Tier.

Doubleshot grants a character a chance to do double (or higher) damage with their ranged or thrown weapon any time they make an attack. Doubleshot applies only to ranged and thrown attacks. (The similar mechanic for melee is Doublestrike.)

If your Doubleshot is greater than 100%, then a second shot is guaranteed, and the remainder is the chance at additional shots. For example:

If your Doubleshot chance is 100%, there's a 100% chance of doing double damage.
If your Doubleshot chance is 120%, you have a 20% chance of doing triple damage instead of double damage.

All damage is multiplied, including base damage, sneak attack, imbue, or weapon enchantments.

Doubleshot values only scale at 50% past a soft cap of 200% (a Doubleshot value of 230%, for example, would only provide 215% chance to Doubleshot.)

Overview[edit]

Ranged Power, introduced in Update 23, is similar in effect to Spell Power and increases straight weapon damage in addition to certain static effects from enhancements. It can be gained from certain feats, enhancements, epic destinies, items and spells.

Applies to all ranged and thrown weapons.
Ranged Power is not applied to static damage riders like a bow's fire damage enchantment, but can apply to some Imbues.
Ranged Sneak Attack damage benefits even more from ranged power, at 150% effectiveness.

The ranged power rating provides a percentage adjustment to straight damage identical to that for melee power (refer to following chart):

Positive ratings use the formula: Damage * (100 + Rating / 100)
For example, a character with 20 ranged power that rolls 100 damage with a ranged weapon will actually deal a total of 100 * 1.2 = 120 damage
Negative ratings use a different formula: Damage * 100 / (100 - Rating)
This is more favorable than the positive rating formula would be for negative ratings
-50 rating means a character deals 100 / (100 - -50) = 100 / (100+50) = 100/150, or 2/3, or 66% damage
-100 rating = half damage (50%)
etc.

Viktranium Experiment crafting is a Heroic (ML 8) and Legendary (ML34) crafting system introduced in Update 75: The Chill of Ravenloft.

The system is very similar to Dinosaur Bone crafting: Collect ingredients, craft them into specially typed Lamordia augments, and place those augments into special augment slots.

Crafting[edit]

Interact with the workbenches in Ludendorf City Hall (one workbench each for the Heroic and Legendary systems) to access the barter window to create Lamordia augments and Calamitous weapons. To slot a Lamordia augment into a Lamordia augment slot, use the Augment Item UI accessible through your Inventory Panel.

Lamordia Augments[edit]

There are 4 Lamordia augment types, each requiring ingredients from different locations (the Set Bonus slot found on Dinosaur Bone items is here replaced by Sun and Moon Augment Slots):

Melancholic (Quests)
Dolorous (Quests)
Miserable (Wilderness)
Woeful (Raid or special Wilderness encounters)

These augment types can come in multiple variants: Armor, Weapon, and Accessory. Each augment type can only fit into an augment slot that exactly matches its type. For example, a "Melancholic (Weapon)" augment cannot go in a "Melancholic Slot (Armor)" slot, only a "Melancholic Slot (Weapon)" slot. You can check which augment type an augment is by looking at its tooltip for the Augment Type row, and then match it to the tooltip of the item you are trying to augment.

Note that Heroic slots will accept Legendary augments (and vice versa) but a Legendary augment will increase the minimum level of a heroic item to 34.

Items with Lamordia augment slots[edit]
C:Viktranium Experiment Crafting items
Most standard named items from The Chill of Ravenloft have a Melancholic and Dolorous Lamordia augment slot. Certain items (including Rare items and Minor Artifacts) additionally have a Miserable Slot. Calamitous Weapons have all three slots, plus a Woeful slot on Legendary only. Downcast Armor items are the only items to possess a Woeful (Accessory) slot.

Unslotting[edit]

Lamordia augments can be unslotted or overwritten:

You can destroy a currently-slotted augment by placing a new augment into the slot.
You can unslot a Lamordia augment by using a Viktranium Pick, which is a rare ingredient found within chests in the Chill of Ravenloft pack or purchased in the DDO Store. This process returns the augment to you unharmed so that you may slot it in a new location. Viktranium Picks may also be found as rewards from Chill of Ravenloft's Sagas. A Jeweler's Tool Kit will not work.
