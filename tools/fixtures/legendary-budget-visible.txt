This derived subtotal buys one new blank and one of each selected Legendary Weapon fill above: Melancholic Flames, Dolorous Flames, Miserable Dimlight and Woeful Salt. It uses the retained crafting revision 720728, not a fresh availability check. Calamitous: 175 each of Legendary Bleak Alternators, Legendary Bleak Conductors, Legendary Bleak Insulators and Legendary Bleak Resistors; 100 Legendary Bleak Wires; 50 Bleak Mementos. Dimlight lists the 100 Wires; Salt lists the 50 Mementos. Flames and Dimlight use the ordinary Legendary Weapon recipes; Salt uses the Wicked Weapon recipe, not the ordinary Woeful recipe that lists Transformers. The retained page links both “Bleak Mementos” and “Legendary Bleak Mementos” to the same ingredient. This covers only those five purchases’ listed ingredients. It excludes colored augments, recovery tools, optional alternative fills and other kit crafts. An unlisted cost is not a guaranteed zero; this is not a complete-cost or current-availability promise.
