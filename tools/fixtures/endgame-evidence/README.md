`4e94fb1274da0d4ffdbe105da382b8ddf9df43b7`, tree

Privacy-redacted derivatives of the November 1–2, 2026 observations are in
`retained/`. `provenance.json` records the public byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes;
`sources.json` contains the selected item/rule observations and actual revision
links when retained. `inventory.json` freezes the target guide's 14 equipped
rows, not an earlier alternative optimizer recipe. Eight primary item records
have revision receipts and six do not. Missing revision links remain missing.

A separate browser pass on 5 November is preserved as a privacy-redacted
derivative in `fresh/`, with its own `fresh-provenance.json`. Its 21 timestamped captures add
primary item pins for Earth and Sky (633073), the Cord (702822) and the Quiver
(630281), leaving the Pendant, Compass and level-36 cloak without receipts here.
The original eight/six historical pin coverage and selected inventory are unchanged.
Blunted Ammunition (612646) supplies an added bludgeoning damage type; this is
not a claim of extra damage dice or complete weapon interactions. Capture text
may include advertising, which is not game evidence. No in-game test was made.
These observations do not certify complete stacking, ownership or availability.
The tests distinguish historical solver output from the listed filled recipe,
preserve all equipment/fills and the complete class/feat/AP/DP sections, and
reject incorrect socket labels and unsupported categorical wording. They do not
change enhancement purchases or certify their timing.

## Public evidence privacy and attribution

Editor names, account links and IP addresses from edit-history metadata, plus
unnecessary review/process records, are omitted from the public copies. The
complete original captures are retained privately. `public-redaction.json` maps
original and public file hashes; the public manifests never describe these
derivatives as byte-identical originals. Article mechanics, exact quotations,
revision links and observation timestamps are preserved. Embedded historical
component hashes are not hashes of the public derivatives.

Source text is from [DDO Wiki](https://ddowiki.com/) contributors under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/).
Each capture links its original article and, when observed, its revision; those
source pages provide contributor history and attribution. No in-game mechanics
verification is implied by these receipts.
