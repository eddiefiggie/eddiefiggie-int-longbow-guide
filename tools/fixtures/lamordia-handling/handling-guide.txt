Before changing a Lamordia augment Use this checklist when filling the heroic Calamitous or changing the crafted endgame bow. The retained crafting page (revision 720728) distinguishes slot compatibility, item level and recovery: Match the full slot type. Compare the augment tooltip’s Augment Type with the item’s slot. The family and variant must match: Melancholic (Weapon) fits Melancholic Slot (Weapon), not Melancholic Slot (Armor). Use Weapon augments for the bow’s Lamordia weapon slots. Keep a levelling bow usable. Heroic slots accept Legendary augments and vice versa, but a Legendary augment raises a heroic item’s minimum level to 34. For the heroic Calamitous route, choose the heroic versions in the recipe above; fitting the slot alone does not make an augment suitable for your level. Recover before replacing if you want to keep the old augment. Placing a new augment in an occupied slot can destroy the slotted augment. A Viktranium Pick removes a Lamordia augment and returns it unharmed for use elsewhere; a Jeweler’s Tool Kit does not work. Check that you have the Pick before changing a fill you intend to keep. Finding a Pick: the retained page lists rare finds in Chill of Ravenloft chests, DDO Store purchase, and possible Chill of Ravenloft Saga rewards. It does not give a drop rate or guarantee a reward. These are retained crafting instructions, not a fresh live-game check or a claim about ordinary colored augment slots.
