# Build principles

These are choices in the [released Guide](../../index.html), not claims of universal DDO optimality.

- Preserve the INT longbow/trapper reincarnation identity: Rogue 1 / Ranger 9 with ten levels of the past-life class on the class-life route. Keep the documented racial and iconic exceptions distinct.
- Preserve selected feat identities and the past-life sequence. A prerequisite or timing correction needs explicit source evidence and reconciliation of repeated instructions; an alternate chassis is a separate design choice.
- Keep bow performance, survival and trap utility in the stated build context. An optimizer's equipment ranking is not a measurement of in-game DPS.
- Use the published gear kit and its stated Salt requirement as the reference. Do not substitute an unmerged gear proposal or assume owned equipment, auction stock or event availability.
- Distinguish direct source statements, arithmetic, build recommendations and uncertainty. Forum suggestions are questions to investigate, not mechanics evidence.

See [Decisions](DECISIONS.md) before extending scope. These principles do not resolve missing source evidence or certify all of the Guide's existing mechanics.
